## Instant answers

What is instant answers?

QuestionPro makes it easy to choose high-quality respondents in the shortest amount of time by letting you purchase QuestionPro audience sample directly from the platform for a single question survey. With just three simple clicks in the QuestionPro Audience tab, you can access over 22 million double opt-in panelists around the globe.

How do I access instant answers?

- Go to Audience

- Click on Create Project button.

- Select Instant Answers from the pop-up window.

- Fill the form

Name:- Enter the name for your project.

- Question:- Type the question you want to ask the respondents.

- Answer options:- Insert all the options one below the other.

- Question type:- Select the question type as single select of multiselect from the drop down button.

- Country:- Select the country where you want to launch this project.

- Number of responses:- Select the number of responses required from the available options or insert the custom number.

Launch:- Hit the Launch button to launch the project.

Your survey is now launched and you can monitor the progress from the dashboard.

How do I pause or close the project after it has been launched?

Once you launched the survey you can pause the project and relaunch it or close it.

Please note, there could be respondents still answering the survey after it has been paused.
