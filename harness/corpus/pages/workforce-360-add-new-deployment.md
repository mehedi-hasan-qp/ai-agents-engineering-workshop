## 360 Add new deployment (Ongoing and Complete)

Add New Deployment" lets you include new participants in a 360 survey. Participants receive the survey to complete, while evaluators give feedback on them. This feature allows you to update the survey by adding more people, ensuring everyone involved is included for feedback.

Click to download video

To Add New Deployments

- Login: Workforce >> Select 360 Survey >> Distribute Tab >> Ongoing >> Click on “+ Add New Deployment”

- On the Add new deployment pop up enter the Name for the deployment and click on “ADD”

Ongoing Tab

- Under the Ongoing Tab once the deployment is listed, you will have access to the following details:

- Deployment Name: Name of the deployment

- Total Subjects: This refers to the total number of participants and evaluators who will be taking part in the feedback survey.

- Status: Shows what status is the deployment currently in

- Responses: Shows how many responses we have collected

- Date: Shows when the Deployment was created

- Response Rate: Shows the percentage of responses collected.

- On the right, you'll find the Kebab Menu (three dots), which allows you to either close the deployment or edit its name.

- Click on the Deployment Name to Add Subjects

- Check the box on the right side of the deployment to export participants to an Excel file.

Completed Tab

- Once the deployment is complete and we have gathered all the necessary feedback, administrators may proceed to close the deployment.

- To Close the deployment, click on the kebab menu (3 Dots) on the right and select “Close Deployment”

- On the Close deployment click on “Yes” to close

- The 360 deployment is completed successfully.

- It will now move to the completed section.

- In the Completed section, should the admin wish to reopen the deployment and revert it to ongoing status, they can click the three dots (kebab menu) on the right and select "Reopen Deployment."

- On the Re- Open Deployment popup click “Yes”

- The Deployment moves back into the “Ongoing” Status tab
