## 360 category header

Grouping questions under specific headers means organizing related questions into clear sections based on their themes or topics. This method makes feedback structured, coherent, and easy to understand, giving a detailed and nuanced view of the surveyed area. For example, in a workforce survey, questions about teamwork, communication, leadership, and job satisfaction can be grouped under their own headers. This improves the survey's clarity and makes it easier to analyse, as responses can be segmented and compared across different categories.

To Set up 360 Category Headers, Go to:

- Login >> Workforce >> Employee Roster >> Admin >> 360 Category Headers >> Add New Header

- A pop appears to add headers one per line

- After adding the headers click on “Create Category Headers”

- Here you can see that 360 Category Headers have been listed successfully

- To Edit a 360 Category Header, click on “Edit Header”. Change/ Edit Header name and click on “update”

- To Delete a 360 Category Header, click on the circled “x” mark. Delete Item pops up then click on “Yes”

To Add 360 Category Header’s to Survey questions, Go to:

- Login workforce >> Select the survey >> Click the Kebab menu (3dots) >> Edit Section

- Click on Rows >> Under Category Headers >> Assign 360 Headers to each question

- Once you have assigned 360 Headers to all questions click on “Update”

- You can click "Preview" to see how the 360 Category Header looks on the survey.
