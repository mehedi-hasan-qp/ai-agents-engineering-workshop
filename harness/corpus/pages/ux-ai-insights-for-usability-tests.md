## AI Insights

How does AI help analyze my usability tests?

QuestionPro UX integrates advanced AI models to accelerate your analysis phase. Instead of watching hours of footage, you can use AI to generate automated summaries of participant behavior and sentiment.

What are the key features of AI Insights?

- Automated Summaries: AI generates high-level overviews of each participant's session, highlighting main takeaways.

- Sentiment Analysis: The system identifies emotional peaks and frustrations throughout the video.

- Auto-Annotations: Key events and task completions are automatically flagged with timestamps for quick review.

You can enable AI analysis during the "Post-test" setup or directly from the video dashboard for all unmoderated sessions.
