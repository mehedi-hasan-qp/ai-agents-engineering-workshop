## Gauge Widget

Introduction

The Gauge chart is a powerful visualization tool designed to display and track essential metrics, such as Mean, NPS (Net Promoter Score), and CES (Customer Effort Score). It presents these metrics in a clear and concise gauge-style dial format, making it easy for users to quickly understand and interpret the data.

This visual representation is particularly valuable for dashboards and executive reporting, where stakeholders need to grasp key performance indicators at a glance. By providing a visually engaging and informative representation of critical metrics, the Gauge feature enhances data-driven decision-making and facilitates effective communication of results.

Purposes and Benefits of Gauge Widget

- Visualize essential scores in real-time using gauges.

- Add context to metrics through colors and thresholds.

- Improve readability of KPIs for quick decision-making.

What are the core functionalities of the Gauge feature?

- Dial Display :

Radial Gauge Visualization: Displays the score on a circular gauge.

- Needle Animation: Choose between static or animated needle for added engagement.

- Threshold Configuration :

Color-Coded Ranges: Assign colors to specific score ranges.

- Performance Indicators: Add markers for key thresholds like low, medium, and high performance.

- Metric Options :

Mean: Calculates the average of numeric responses.

- Net Promoter Score (NPS): Measures loyalty by subtracting detractors from promoters.

- Title and Label Controls :

Customization: Add titles and labels to describe data.

- Tooltips: Provide additional context when hovering over chart elements.

Glossary of Gauge-related terms

NPS (Net Promoter Score) : Percentage of promoters minus detractors.

CES (Customer Effort Score) : Average score of how easy customers found a task.

Thresholds : Value ranges used to color segments of the gauge.
