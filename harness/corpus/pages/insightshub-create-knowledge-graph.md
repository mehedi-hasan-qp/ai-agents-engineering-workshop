## Create a knowledge graph

Experience the power of Knowledge Graphs to map out and visualize the intricate connections between your Projects and broader research themes or concepts.

Kickstart your journey by crafting relevant Knowledge Graphs aligned with your team's research focus. Next, seamlessly integrate Key Findings from your Projects into these Graphs. As you accumulate Key Findings within your Knowledge Graphs, a vivid picture of interrelated themes emerges.

Through the strategic linking of Key Findings to diverse Knowledge Graphs, uncover hidden research gaps or unexpected relationships that could potentially fuel innovation and drive strategic insights for your marketing and product endeavors.

How to create a Knowledge graph?

If you are creating the knowledge graph for the first time, you will see a button "New Knowledge Graph". Click on it and you will be good to go for creating your 1st knowledge graph.

In case, it's your second or more, you can click on the "+" button.
