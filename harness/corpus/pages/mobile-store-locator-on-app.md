## Store locator on QuestionPro Offline App

The store locator is designed to help you find the nearest store or outlet. It allows you to find locations of the business within proximity of an address or within a selected region.

To see how to add the store locator question in to the survey click here.

This question type can also be used in our QuestionPro Offline App.

Note: Please turn ON the Online connect option in your QuestionPro Offline App.

Store Locator type question works only if QuestionPro Offline App is connected to internet.
