## Synthetic cohort

What is Synthetic cohort?

A Synthetic cohort is built from your community/survey data that allows you to generate insights without launching a new study.
It acts as a research workspace where:

- Your selected data source is organized into a cohort

- AI synthesizes past responses

- You can ask a set of questions to generate insights

Think of a Synthetic cohort as a reusable research group created from your existing data.

How to Create a Synthetic cohort?

Follow these steps:

- Go to the Synthetic platform

- Select data source: Choose the community (or dataset) you want to use.

- Click “New research”

- Define your cohort:

Name :- Provide a name to your cohort

- Short description : Add a brief summary.

- Cohort purpose : Provide a detailed cohort purpose: The system will use this purpose to filter the data from the community and create a cohort to run different research modules like IDI and Converse AI .

Example:

Understand why customers churn

- Explore perceptions about pricing

- Evaluate onboarding experience

- Set a similarity cut-off percentage: Choose the similarity percentage that determines how closely members should match your cohort purpose.

Higher % (e.g., 80–90%): More precise match, smaller but highly relevant cohort

- Lower % (e.g., 50–70%): Broader match, larger cohort with more diversity

- Save and generate: Click Create

Your Synthetic cohort is now ready to use for insight generation.

What can I do with my Synthetic Cohort?

Post-creation, access two main features: In-Depth Interviews (IDI) and Converse AI .

- In-Depth Interviews (IDI)

Select cohort members (1-50) based on matching scores to your study purpose.

- Add or AI-generate questions.

- Run interviews → Get AI responses, key takeaways, summaries, quotes, similarities/differences, and action recommendations.

- Download report as PDF or Markdown.

- Converse AI

Engage in real-time, conversational interviews with cohort members.

- Ask dynamic follow-up questions based on responses.

- Ideal for exploratory research, probing deeper insights conversationally.

- Generate summaries or export chat transcripts.

Both preserve privacy—no real data is used beyond synthesis, and results stay in your account.
