## Billing and Profile Management

How do I update my personal profile and time zone?

Your profile contains the core administrative details for your account. To access these settings, click on the My profile icon in the bottom-left navigation bar.

- Information: You can view your account email and edit your name, phone number, and company name.

- Time Zone: It is important to set your correct local time zone to ensure all test schedules and "Last modified" timestamps are accurate to your location.

How do I manage my payment methods?

The Billing section allows you to manage how you pay for your research credits and licenses.

- Payment Profiles: Click + Add a new card to securely save a credit card to your account for future purchases.

- Transaction History: This table provides a complete audit trail of your purchases, including the Item (e.g., Enterprise license), Description, Quantity, and the total Amount billed.

- Invoices and Receipts: You can download individual invoices or receipts for your records by clicking the download icon in the respective columns.

Where can I view my plan details and seat usage?

Scroll to the bottom of the profile page to view your Plan details . This section highlights:

- Plan Type: Displays your current subscription (e.g., ENTERPRISE Yearly) and allows you to initiate a plan change.

- Renewal/Cancellation: Displays the date of your next invoice or account expiration.

- Additional Charges (Seats): View how many seats are currently used within your organization. Under Enterprise plans, you typically have access to unlimited seats at no additional charge.
