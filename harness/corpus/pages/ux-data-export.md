## Exporting Your Data: Usability tests, Interviews and Focus groups

How do I export my test data?

QuestionPro UX allows you to export your qualitative and quantitative data into a CSV format for more in-depth analysis or for use in your own reporting tools.

To export data for a specific methodology, navigate to the results page of your test. You will find the Export CSV button at the top of the data table. This file includes participant demographics, task completion rates, and survey responses.
