## Assessment - Vendor Score

How to view vendor score?

To view vendor score:

- Click on the assessment link under a portal.

- Click on the list view to see the vendor score.

Can I view vendor details in this page?

Yes, you can view vendor details. To view vendor details, click on vendor name hyperlink. User can also modify details of the vendor.

Can I view document attached by vendor?

Yes, you can view documents attached by vendor. Click on the hyperlink in document to view the attached document. Documents given in bullet points are document not attached.

Can I view section wise score for a vendor?

Yes, you can view section wise score for a vendor. To view section wise score:

- Click on the score hyperlink.

- View section wise score and document. To download attached document, hover on the listed document and click.
