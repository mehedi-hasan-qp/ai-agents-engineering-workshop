## BI - Exports

Introduction

The Exports feature within the QuestionPro platform provides a comprehensive solution for users seeking to analyze their survey data. By offering compatibility with widely adopted formats like SPSS (.sav) and CSV (.csv), Exports ensures seamless integration with a broad array of statistical and analytical tools. This functionality empowers users to delve deeper into their survey results, extracting valuable insights and facilitating data-driven decision-making.

Purpose and Benefits:

- Streamline Data Analysis: Effortlessly transfer your survey data into a format that's primed for analysis, eliminating manual formatting and reducing the risk of errors.

- Ensure Broad Compatibility: Export your data in widely accepted formats that seamlessly integrate with popular statistical software packages like SPSS, R, and SAS, as well as spreadsheet programs like Excel.

- Accelerate Insights: Save valuable time and effort by generating clean, well-structured data exports that are ready for immediate use in your analysis tools, allowing you to focus on extracting meaningful insights.

Core Functionality

Export Format Selection
CSV:
This widely compatible format can be opened and edited in various spreadsheet software, including Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers, and OpenOffice Calc. It's ideal for data analysis, manipulation, and sharing.

SPSS:
Specifically designed for statistical analysis, this format is compatible with IBM SPSS Statistics software. It preserves valuable metadata such as variable labels and value codes, ensuring data integrity and facilitating in-depth analysis within SPSS.
