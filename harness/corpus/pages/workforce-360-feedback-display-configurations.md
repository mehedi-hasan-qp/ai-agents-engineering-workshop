## 360 Feedback - Display Configurations

360 feedback display configuration allows you to enable/disable a particular type of reviewer from reviewing the employees. For example, if you disable peers, then the peers of the selected employee will not be able to review him

Following configurations are available to enable/disable a reviewer type:

- Disable "Self" to disable Self review.

- Disable "Peers" to disable Peer review.

- Disable "Supervisor" to disable Direct Report reviewing Supervisor.

- Disable "Direct Report" to disable Supervisor reviewing Direct Report.

- Disable "External Reviewer" to disable External Reviewer review.

How can I change display configuration?

To change display configuration, go to:

Login >> Workforce >> 360 feedback survey

- Click on Settings .

- Enable/disable the display configuration and click on Save
