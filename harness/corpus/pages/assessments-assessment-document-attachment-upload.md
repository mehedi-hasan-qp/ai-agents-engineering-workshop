## Assessment - Document Attachment Upload

How do I attach a document for an assessment?

You can attach a document to an assessment once you complete your assessment survey on the portal. Upload document for the required section/question. To upload the document:

- Login to vendor portal.

- Respond to the assessment survey.

- Click on Document Attachment hyperlink given under section.

- Upload document against selected response.

Can I delete an uploaded document?

Yes, you can delete an uploaded document. To delete, click on the Delete option against the document uploaded. A pop-up window will appear with a comment Are you sure you want to delete this doc? . Click on Ok to delete.
