## Assessment - Create Portal

How do I create a Portal?

You can create a portal by clicking on the “Add Portal” icon. You can give the portal a name, a URL, add a logo and create a welcome message for the vendor portal.

Can I delete a portal?

Yes, you can delete a portal. To delete a portal, click on the configure icon then click delete icon.

Can I edit the content of a portal?

Yes, you can edit the content of a portal. To edit the contents of a portal:

- Click on the portal's name.

- Go to content and click on the item you want to edit.

- Update the details and save the changes.
