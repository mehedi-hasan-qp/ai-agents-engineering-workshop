## Voice recognition for offline app

What is voice recognition?

Voice recognition is an interdisciplinary sub field of computational linguistics that develops methodologies and technologies that enables the recognition and translation of spoken language into text by computers. It is also known as automatic speech recognition, computer speech recognition or speech to text.
