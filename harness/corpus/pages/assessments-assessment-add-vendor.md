## Assessment - Add Vendor

How do I add a vendor to a portal?

To add a vendor to a portal:

- Click on the portal's name.

- Click on the add vendor button.

- Fill in the details of the vendor and click save.

Can I edit the vendor details?

Yes, you can edit vendor details. To edit vendor details:

- Click on the portal name.

- Click on the email address hyperlink.

- Update the details and save the changes.

Can I add/remove fields from vendor creation form?

Yes, you can add/remove custom fields in vendor creation form. To add/remove custom fields:

- Click on the portal name.

- Go to profile tab and click on add new profile field.

- Add description of profile field and save.

- Click on delete icon to delete a field from vendor registration form.

Can I modify type of profile field?

Yes, you can modify type of profile field. To modify the profile field:

- Click on the profile field label.

- Update the profile field type.
