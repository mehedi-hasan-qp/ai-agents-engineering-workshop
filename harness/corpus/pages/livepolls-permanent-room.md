## Using Permanent URLs/Rooms with LivePolls

The Permanent Room feature in LivePolls allows users to host polls using a stable, permanent URL. This URL ensures that the poll remains active until the user decides to turn it off. Users can effortlessly share this generated URL with their target respondents, providing them continuous access to the poll for the desired duration.

How to enable and use the Permanent Room feature:

Follow these simple steps to enable the Permanent Room feature for your LivePoll:

Login to your QuestionPro account using your credentials.

Once logged in, navigate to the LivePolls section from the main dashboard.

Create a new poll from scratch or choose from our collection of ready-to-use poll templates to streamline your process.

Enable Permanent Room: After finishing the poll setup and edits, locate the toggle switch labeled "Permanent Room." Turn it on to activate the permanent URL feature.

Copy and Share URL: Once the Permanent Room feature is enabled, a 'Copy URL' button will appear. Click on it to copy the generated permanent URL to your clipboard. Share this URL with your target respondents for uninterrupted access to the poll.

Real-time Analytics: Monitor the performance of your poll in real-time by using the drop-down menu to switch between questions. Access comprehensive analytics for each question within the poll, helping you gather valuable insights.
