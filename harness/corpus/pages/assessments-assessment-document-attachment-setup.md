## Assessment - Document Attachment Setup

How do I setup a Document Attachment for a question in an assessment?

To enable Document Attachment for a question in an assessment, follow the steps below:

- Login >> Assessment>>Add New Assessment OR Click on existing Assessment

- Go to More>> Attach Document Setting for a question.

- Give Title for the attachment and click on Check Box if document is Required .

- Click on Save .
