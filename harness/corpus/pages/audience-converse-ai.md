## Converse AI

What is Converse AI?

Converse AI lets you have a live, interactive conversation with your synthetic cohort inside a workbook.
Instead of asking all your questions at once, you can ask follow-up questions, explore insights dynamically, and save the entire conversation for later use.

Think of it as talking to your data — like conducting a virtual interview with your synthetic audience.

When should you use Converse AI?

Use Converse AI when you want to:

- Ask “why” or “how” questions after seeing initial insights.

- Explore customer motivations, emotions, or pain points.

- Validate assumptions without launching a new study.

- Dive deeper into unexpected results.

- Simulate qualitative interviews or CX conversations.

How do I start Converse AI?

Once you have created a workbook by defining the workbook purpose and setting the similarity cut-off percentage, follow the steps below:

- Step 1: Open a workbook

Select Converse AI research mode

- Step 2: Launch Converse AI

Click on one of the “Member card” to start the conversation.

- A chat panel will open inside the workbook

- Step 3: Ask your first question

Select a question from pre-populated set of questions OR

- Type a question in natural language

Your chat has now been initiated with this member, and you can continue with your follow-up questions.

Can I initiate multiple chats with a single member?

Yes, you can start multiple chats with a single member. To do so:

- Click on the member to open a new chat window and start a new chat

OR

- Click the “+” icon to open a new chat window.

Where can I find the chat history?

There are two types of chat history:

- Conversation history:
  This is the history of conversations initiated with any member. You can find it on the Converse AI research module page, as shown below.

- Recent: This shows the history of all chats initiated with a single member. You can find it within the member’s conversation or chat interface.
