## Demographic Filtering

How can I filter my participant audience?

When recruiting from the QuestionPro UX panel, you can apply detailed demographic filters to ensure you are getting feedback from your target market.

Available filters include:

- Age and Gender

- Country and Region

- Household Income and Employment Status

- Education Level and Family Status

- Social Network usage and Community Type

These filters help you narrow down a broad panel to the specific segment that matches your user persona.
