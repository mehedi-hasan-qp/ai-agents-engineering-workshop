## Creating Highlight Reels(Usability tests and Interviews)

What is a Highlight Reel?

A highlight reel is a compilation of curated video clips from one or multiple sessions. It is an effective way to showcase patterns in user behavior or present specific insights to stakeholders in a concise format.

How do I create a highlight reel?

To build your reel, first create video annotations or "clips" within your test results. Once you have saved your clips, navigate to the Video Analysis section. Select the clips you wish to include and click Create Highlight Reel . You can reorder clips to tell a compelling story about your research findings.
