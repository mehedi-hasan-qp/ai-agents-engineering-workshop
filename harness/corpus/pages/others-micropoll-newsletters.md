## Email Newsletters - Adding and Embedded Dymanic Controls

Dynamic Controls are interactive components that can be placed on a Newsletter. Currently we offer the following dynamic controls:

- MicroPoll - Quick Single Question Embedded Polls

- RSS Feed - If you have an RSS Feed, the newsletter can contain the first 3 topics in the RSS Feed

How do I an Inline Poll to my Newsletter?

Adding polls to newsletters and email marketing is acheived by using the MicroPoll tool. To add a poll to your email newsletter or HTML emails:

- Login &raquo; My MicroPolls (Select Poll)
