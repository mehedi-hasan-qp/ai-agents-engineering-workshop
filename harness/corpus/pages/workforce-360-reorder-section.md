## 360 Reorder section

Reorder section in a 360 survey allows participants to prioritize or arrange items in a specific order according to their preferences or importance. This helps gather insights on what respondents consider most significant or relevant.

Click to download video

To Reorder Section

- Click On the Kebab menu (Three dots) on the right of the Section Header/Name and select Reorder Section.

- On the reorder section popup, you can drag and drop sections to arrange them how you want.
