## Data Sharing Options

Introduction

Data Sharing Options is a feature available in the Community > Settings that enables community managers to enhance transparency and engagement among members. By activating this feature, community admin could share live updates on activities (similar to a live ticker), display the composition of community member profiles, and publish a leadership board. These real-time insights foster a sense of competition, recognition, and deeper connection within the community.

The purpose of Data Sharing Options is to keep members informed and motivated by highlighting ongoing activities, showcasing community demographics, and celebrating top contributors.

Core Functionality

Core Functions

- Activity Stream:
  Displays real-time updates of activities happening across the community directly in the member portal. This allows members to see when others join events, complete tasks, or achieve milestones, driving momentum and engagement.
  Setting > Data Sharing Options > Activity stream

Activity Steam on Community Member Profile

- DataShare™:
  Shares aggregate, anonymized information about the profile of the community, such as member demographics, aggregated locations, or other profile attributes. This gives members a sense of belonging and helps them understand the diversity and strengths of the community.
  Setting > Data Sharing Options > DataShareTM

DataShare™ on Community Member Profile

- LeaderBoard:
  Publicly displays a leaderboard ranking members based on points earned through community activities. Members can view their own standing and see the activities that contribute to point accumulation, such as completing surveys, participating in discussions, or referring new members.
  Setting > Data Sharing Options > LeaderBoard

LeaderBoard on Community Member Profile

Glossary

Glossary Terms

- Community Portal: The online platform where members access and engage with the community.

- Live Ticker: A continuously updating feed that shows live events or activities as they happen.

- Leadership Board (Leaderboard): A ranking of members based on their participation and point earnings in the community.

- Profile Composition: Aggregate summary data showing demographics or attributes of the community membership.

- Points System: A gamified scoring system that awards members points for completing designated activities within the community.

- Referral: The act of inviting new members to join the community, often rewarded with points.
