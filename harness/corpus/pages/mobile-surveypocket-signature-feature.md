## Signature Question Type

The signature feature in QuestionPro Offline App allows for you to collect your survey participant's signature. This feature can be enabled by you when you create your survey on SurveyAnalytics.com

To enable signature collection, you'll first need to create a new question under

Add New Question >> Advanced Question Types >> Attach/Upload File.

When you use QuestionPro Offline App on your device, you will now see the signature feature integrated into your survey.
