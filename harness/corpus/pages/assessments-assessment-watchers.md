## Assessment - Setting up watchers list

Watchers are typically stakeholders and business stakeholders that want to on-board a vendor. Adding watcher to a vendor enables the user to get notified when a vendor starts or completes his assessment.

How do I set up an Action Alert?

- Login >> Assessment

- Click on the assessment link added in a portal.

- Click on list view menu.

- Click on the vendor name hyperlink to assign watcher.

- Click on add watcher button to enter watcher details.

- Enter watcher email address and click on save to add watcher for the vendor.
