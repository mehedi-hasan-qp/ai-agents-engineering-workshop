## Assessment - Add Score

How do I add a score to a question?

You can define scores against answer options for each question. To define score against questions follow these steps:

- Select question to define score against it.

- Click on Logic.

- Go to scoring.

- Enter score against each answer option.

How to add Score range?

You can define score range to determine if the vendor falls in high risk, low risk or medium risk. To add a score range:

- Click on “Score Range”.

- Click on “Add Score Range”.

- Define labels and score range.

Can I delete a defined score range?

Yes, you can delete defined score range. Click on delete option against the defined score range.
