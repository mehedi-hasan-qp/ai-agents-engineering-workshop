## View Voting Trends - How to?

To enable/disable Voting Trends (Time Based) on my MicroPoll:

Goto:

- Login &raquo; My MicroPolls (Select Poll) &raquo; Edit MicroPoll

Click on the Advanced Results Settings collapse menu and select the needed chart type from the Voting Trends Chart menu. Click on the Save Changes button to update the changes..

Where can I see the Voting Trend Charts

When you enable Voting Trend Charts on the MicroPoll, Respondents can see the charts as soon as they vote.

You can also see these charts under:

- Login &raquo; My MicroPolls (Select Poll) &raquo; Reports
