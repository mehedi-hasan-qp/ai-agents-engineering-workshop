## Partner Apps

What is the QuestionPro Partnerships Ecosystem?

The QuestionPro Partnerships Ecosystem is a collaborative network designed to enhance research capabilities by connecting users with industry experts, AI-powered tools, and specialized analytics providers.
Key benefits include:

- On-Demand Expertise: Access to strategists and industry specialists.

- AI Agility: Deploy predictive analytics and smart automation to stay ahead.

- Global Reach: A curated network of partners across various industries like Healthcare, Automotive and more.

For more details on the partnership program, you can visit the Partnerships Page .

How can I create an OAuth App in QuestionPro?

To securely integrate your application with QuestionPro using OAuth 2.0, follow these steps:

- Log in to your QuestionPro Partnerships account.

- Click on the App tab.

- Click on New OAuth 2.0 App button.

- Enter the name of the app and click on Create OAuth 2.0 App .

- Once the app is created, you will receive a Client ID and Client Secret . Ensure you store the Client Secret securely, as it will not be shown again for security reasons.

- Enter the Display Text and the Callback URL (this must be a secure HTTPS URL where the user is sent after authorization) and click Save .

- Once you save the app it is available under App . You can edit the app by clicking on the app.

How do I manage an OAuth 2.0 App?

You can manage your existing applications by returning to the Apps section.
From here, you can click on the app to update your App information such as Callback URLs and Description.

You can also change the status of your app by changing the toggle between Active and Inactive.

FAQs

- Can I use an HTTP URL for the Callback URI?

No. For security purposes, QuestionPro requires all OAuth Redirect URIs to use the HTTPS protocol.

- Is there a limit to how many OAuth apps I can create?

No. All our partners can create multiple applications.
