## AI-Generated Executive Summaries

This AI-powered tool automatically analyzes all participant videos, transcripts, and survey responses for a specific test to produce a high-level report. It identifies overarching patterns, primary pain points, and notable successes, allowing you to share insights with stakeholders almost immediately after data collection ends.

How does this work?

Once you have at least 5 completed sessions in a usability test, navigate to the Analytics tab. Click the Generate Executive Summary button. The AI will process the data and provide a structured report including a project overview, key findings, and recommended next steps. You can edit this text directly or export it to your final report.
