## AI Persona Creator – Instantly Create Actionable Customer Personas

What is the QuestionPro AI Persona Creator?

The QuestionPro AI Persona Creator is an AI feature within the Journey Management platform that enables users to generate real-time, dynamic personas using customer feedback, behavioral patterns, and demographic data. These personas help you better understand customer motivations, pain points, and preferences — without manual effort.

How to Access and Use the AI Persona Creator?

- Select the Workspace for which you want to create the personas

- Navigate to the Persona section from the left navigation menu

- Click on the “QuestionPro AI” button next to Add Persona button.

- Enter a brief context or description of the segment you're targeting.

- Select the sections you want to include in the persona (e.g., Goals, Frustrations, Tools).

- Enable or disable the Demographics Toggle based on your need for age, location, job role, etc.

- Click Generate to automatically create the persona.

- Review and edit the generated persona to fit your unique use case.

What Are the Benefits of Using the AI Persona Creator?

- Reduces time spent on manual persona creation.

- Produces data-driven, real-time personas based on actual customer interactions.

- Enhances journey mapping and CX strategy with up-to-date insights.

- Improves personalization across marketing, UX, and product efforts.

Can I Edit a Generated Persona?

Yes. Once a persona is generated, it opens in an editable template. You can revise content in any card to better suit your needs or company context.

Tips for Best Results

- Provide clear and specific context when generating personas.

- Only select sections relevant to your goal to keep personas concise.

- Use generated personas alongside journey maps for better visualization and decision-making.
