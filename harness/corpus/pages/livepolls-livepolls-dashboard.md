## Results and Analytics

Where can I see the dashboard report?

LivePolls dashboard gives you access a comprehensive visual representation of data obtained from every individual poll or quiz session, offering valuable insights and analytics. To access dashboard, go to:

Login » Select LivePoll » Session and Analytics

How do I share or export my results?

Currently, we do not support direct Excel or PDF downloads. To share or view your data, use the unique report link provided in the Session and Analytics tab. This link allows you to access the most up-to-date analytics for your poll or quiz.
