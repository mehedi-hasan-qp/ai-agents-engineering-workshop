## In-Depth Interviews (IDI)

What is In-Depth Interviews (IDI) under the Synthetic platform??

In-Depth Interviews (IDI) is a feature within the Synthetic platform that generates quick qualitative-style insights and a summarized report based on your workbook cohort.
Instead of conducting live interviews, the system synthesizes existing community data to simulate interview-like insights. This helps you generate insights which includes:

- Quick summary

- Key takeaways

- In-depth summary

- Insightful quotes

- Similarities and differences

- Recommended action

Think of it as a fast, AI-powered summary of what in-depth interviews might reveal — using your existing data.

How do I generate IDI reports?

Once you have created a workbook, follow these steps:

- Open a Workbook: Go to the Synthetic platform and open the workbook you want to analyze

- Select “In-Depth Interview (IDI)”: Click on the In-Depth Interviews (IDI) option within the research mode.

- Specify member count: Specify the number of members to include in the interview (between 1 and 50).

- Add questions:

Use QuestionPro AI to auto-generate questions by clicking the Ask question button.

- Or add your own questions.

- Generate report: Click on the Generate report button.

The system will analyze the cohort data by asking all the selected questions to the top selected members and produce quick insights which includes:

- Member responses

- Quick summary

- Key takeaways

- In-depth summary

- Insightful quotes

- Similarities and differences

- Recommended action

You can also download the report in PDF or Markdown format
