## Assessment – Create Assessment

How to create an assessment?

You can create an assessment by adding question directly or by selecting a framework. To create assessment by directly adding question:

- Login to the account.

- Click on “New Assessment”.

- Enter name of the assessment and click on create.

Can I delete an Assessment?

Yes, you can delete or rename an assessment. To delete an assessment, click on delete option provided against the assessment. To rename an assessment, click on rename option provided against the assessment.
