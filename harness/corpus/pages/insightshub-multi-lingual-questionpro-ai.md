## Multilingual QuestionPro AI Chat

QuestionPro AI now understands and responds in multiple languages. You can ask your questions in the language of your choice — for example, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and more — and QuestionPro AI will reply in the same language, while still analyzing your underlying data the same way it always has.

This makes it easier for global teams to interact with their research data in their native language, without needing to translate prompts or responses manually.

How to ask QuestionPro AI a question in another language?

- Go to: Login » InsightsHub » QuestionPro AI .

- Click in the chat input at the bottom of the screen.

- Type your question in the language of your choice. For example, to ask a question in Spanish, type:

- Resume lo que dijo Andrew en la reunión del comité de finanzas y asuntos corporativos.

- Press Enter or click the Send button.

- QuestionPro AI will respond in the same language used in the question, with the same insights, citations, and structure it would provide for an English query.
