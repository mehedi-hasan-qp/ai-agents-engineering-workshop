## 360 Delete Sections

In a 360 survey, the "Delete Section" option allows you to remove an entire section of the survey. A section could include several questions or a specific part of the survey focused on a certain topic.
Example: Let's say you have a section in the survey called "Leadership Skills" with 5 questions. If you decide that you no longer need this part, you can use the "Delete Section" option to remove all 5 questions at once, instead of deleting each question one by one.

Click to download video

To Delete a Section

- Click On the Kebab menu (Three dots) on the right of each Section Header/Name and select the Delete section.

- On the "Delete Section" pop-up, click "Yes" when asked if you're sure you want to delete the question. Keep in mind that if the section is deleted, all related questions and their responses will also be deleted.
