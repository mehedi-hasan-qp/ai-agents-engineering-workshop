## How to create a live feed question for your live polls or quizzes

The live feed question lets you ask the live audience an open-end question and gather responses in real-time. Live feed is available for both polls and quizzes.
Live feed comments offer you a steady stream of feedback while also letting users curate those comments in a democratic manner by voting on their preferred answers.

Ask for suggestions, preferences, or have folks explain their previous answers. Those are just a few examples of what you can do with this open ended question type during your poll or quiz sessions.

How to add a live feedback to a poll or quiz?

To add live feedback to an online poll or quiz

Go to: Login » LivePolls (Select a poll) » Workspace » Click on the blue plus + sign » Live Feed.

Then just write your question.

Host view:

Overview: Shows you what’s happening in real-time.

Live feed: Shows you a continuous stream of all incoming answers by all other participants.

Top votes: Shows the answers that other users have picked as their favorites.

Pinned: Pin any response to the top of the feed so that you don't track of it.

Respondent view

Don't forget to vote: Shows you a continuous stream of user answers. You can also vote any answer.

Top Voted: View top voted answer by the respondents.
