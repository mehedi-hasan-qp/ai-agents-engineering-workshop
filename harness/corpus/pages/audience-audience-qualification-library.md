## Audience Qualification Library

What is audience qualification library?

The audience Qualification Library is a collection of screening questions, filters, and audience definitions that help you easily find the exact respondents you need for your surveys. It consists of qualification criteria based on different categories such as demographics, employment, family, travel, and more. This allows users to check feasibility or launch a survey to a specific target audience by applying these criteria.

How to apply qualifications?

- Go to Create project under Specialized Audience

- Click on Add criteria

- Select the qualification category from the list

- Select the qualification criteria within the selected category.

- Select the options that match your target audience’s requirements, and click the Save button.

Once added, the qualification will update the feasibility based on your selected criteria.
